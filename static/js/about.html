Welcome to BloodPrint! AI-powered fingerprint blood group detection system.

Methodology

Fingerprint images are preprocessed and analyzed using a CNN-based ResNet model to predict blood groups accurately.

Dataset

Total 5000-6000 fingerprint images covering 8 blood groups. Images resized to 256x256 for model training.
